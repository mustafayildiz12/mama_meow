My 5-month-old wakes up every 2 hours at night. Is that normal?

14:05
pets
Meow auto_awesome
Just now

At 5 months, waking every 2 hours is very common and usually linked to the 4-to-6 month sleep regression, emerging developmental leaps, or hunger spurts.

Evaluate wake windows: aim for 2 to 2.5 hours before bedtime.

Ensure full feeds during daytime to reduce nighttime nutritional waking.

Establish a soothing, consistent 15-minute wind-down routine.
